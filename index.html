ABOUT THE REVOLUTION

Educating Minds.
Building Confident Futures.

The Revolution Collegiate (TRC) supports students through academic preparation and concept-based learning.

Our academic areas include Pre-Engineering, Pre-Medical, Computer Science, Commerce, Mathematics, English and other school and college subjects.

We aim to provide students with guidance, regular practice and a learning environment focused on understanding and progress.
